07 — Projections

Estimates, not financial advice

The “Saved so far,” Week, Month, Year, and Lifetime figures in Circuit are simple arithmetic, not a financial product: your daily cost or daily minutes, multiplied over time. The Lifetime figure projects to age 80 using the date of birth you enter, and — as the app states — it assumes you stay clean and your daily cost stays the same.

  • The line is straight, not compounded. It is not an investment return, a savings account, or a forecast of your finances.
  • Actual money and time saved will differ. Costs change, lapses happen, and no projection in Circuit is a guarantee or a promise.
  • Nothing in Circuit is financial, investment, tax, or gambling advice, and nothing in it is a recommendation to place, avoid, or size any bet or trade.
