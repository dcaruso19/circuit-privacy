01 — What Circuit stores

Stored locally — not sent to Circuit

Circuit does not operate a server that receives your streak or journal data. The information you enter is saved in the app’s local storage on your phone so the app can work offline and show your history, trophies, and projections. It is not sent to Circuit. It can be included in a device backup only if you enable iCloud or Google backup — see Section 6.

02 — What we don’t collect

No account, no location, no contacts

Circuit does not collect or request:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number to use the app
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • Health records from Apple Health, Health Connect, or your medical providers
  • Financial account credentials — the money-saved figures are estimates from numbers you type in
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking

If a future feature ever needs one of these, this policy will be updated and the app will ask for permission before it is collected.

03 — How your data is used

Only to run the features you see

Your locally stored data is used only on your device to:

  • Count clean days, streaks, and check-in history
  • Calculate “Saved so far” and projections for Week, Month, Year, and Lifetime — assuming your daily cost stays the same
  • Award XP, unlock trophies, and show trends and patterns
  • Show resources and reminders you have chosen

It is not used for advertising, profiling, research, or training models, and it is not sold to anyone.

04 — Sharing & third parties

We don’t share what we don’t have

Because your logs are not sent to us, we cannot share, rent, or sell them. There are three limited exceptions that involve the platform, not your journal content:

  • Apple & Google: They distribute the app and, if you buy a paid feature in the future, process the payment. They receive the payment information they need — Circuit never sees your full card number. Their privacy policies govern that purchase data.
  • Links you tap: If you tap a resource — for example, calling 988 or opening a clinic or therapy listing — you leave Circuit and that service’s policy applies. Circuit does not send your streak data with you.
  • Support you initiate: If you contact support through the App Store / Play Store listing or by email, we will see what you choose to send in that message. Don’t send passwords or medical records.
05 — Notifications

Local reminders, controlled by you

Circuit’s reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not marketing pushes sent from a server with your journal attached. You can turn them off at any time in Circuit’s settings or in your phone’s system settings, and turning them off does not delete your data.

06 — Storage, backups & security

Protected by your device lock

Your data inherits the protection of your phone — your passcode, Face ID / fingerprint, and device encryption. Keep your device locked and your operating system up to date.

Device backups: If you use iCloud Backup or Google backup, your device backup may include Circuit’s local data, just like other apps. That backup is stored by Apple or Google under your account and your backup settings — you can exclude apps or turn backup off in system settings. Circuit does not receive a copy.

No method of storage is 100% secure. If you share an unlocked device with someone else, they can open the app and see what you have logged.

07 — Your choices & deletion

You don’t need to ask us to delete it

  • Edit or remove entries inside the app where that control is offered (for example, trigger logs in History).
  • Change your date of birth in Settings — it only changes the Lifetime projection.
  • Delete everything on that device by deleting the app’s data in system settings or uninstalling Circuit. Uninstalling removes the locally stored copy on that phone.
  • Backups: To remove Circuit data from an existing device backup, delete or refresh that backup in iCloud / Google settings.

Because we hold no server copy, we cannot recover data you delete and we cannot delete a copy from a phone we don’t have access to — deletion is in your hands, immediately.

08 — Children

Not directed to children under 13

Circuit is intended for adults managing their own habits. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and it does not knowingly collect data from them. Since data is local, a parent or guardian who finds the app on a child’s device can delete it by uninstalling the app.

09 — Health disclaimer

Support, not treatment

Circuit provides self-tracking, education, and encouragement. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for professional care. Resources listed in the app are for information only.

If you are in crisis or may harm yourself, call or text 988 in the United States (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or contact your local emergency services. Talk to your own clinician about treatment decisions, medication, and dosing — Circuit does not provide doses or claim that any technique works for you.

Circuit is not a healthcare provider and is not a “covered entity” under HIPAA. The privacy protection here comes from this policy and from keeping data on your device, not from HIPAA.

10 — Changes & contact

If the app changes, this page changes first

We will update this page and its effective date before launching any feature that changes what is collected or shared — for example, optional accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or subscriptions. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes. Continuing to use Circuit after an update means the revised policy applies to the new version.
