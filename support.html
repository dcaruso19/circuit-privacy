01 — Streaks & start times

How streak counting works

A streak day is a full 24 hours from the moment the streak started. A streak started today at 6:00 pm completes its first day tomorrow at 6:00 pm — the ring fills gradually until then, and the counter reads 0 days in between. That is the counter working correctly, not a bug.

How do I start a new streak?

Add a streak from the app’s add-streak flow and set:

  • The habit name
  • The start date and start time — if the streak starts today, the time defaults to right now; if it started in the past, set the time it actually began
  • The daily cost and daily time the habit used, which power the money and time saved figures

The same start date and time fields are used when streaks are created during onboarding.

My streak shows 0 days but I started hours ago — is it broken?

No. Days count complete 24-hour periods from your start time. A streak started an hour ago shows 0 days with a partly filled ring, and the first day lands exactly 24 hours after the start time you set.

The start time on my streak is wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes — open the streak’s edit screen and correct the start date and start time there. Counts, the ring, and projections update from the corrected timestamp. Streaks created before start times were added keep counting exactly as they did, from the date stored on them.

What happens to my count after a lapse?

Recording a lapse closes the current cycle and starts a new one from the exact moment of the lapse, so the new cycle also runs on true 24-hour periods. Your history, lapse debrief, and triggers stay recorded — a lapse resets the current streak count, not your record.

02 — The savings chart

Reading money and time saved

The chart on Progress draws one line per streak. The solid part is what you have saved so far; the dashed part is the projection if you stay clean, at the daily rate you set for each streak.

How do I see the value on a specific day?

Drag your finger across the chart. A guide line follows it, with a dot on each streak that has started by that day, and a small label on the chart itself shows the date — or, on the Lifetime range, the age — and the total across all streaks for that day. While you scrub, the legend below the chart shows each streak’s value at that day instead of saved so far. The position stays when you lift your finger; changing the range or the Money / Time toggle clears it.

What do the ranges and the Money / Time toggle do?
  • 30 days and 1 year label points by calendar date.
  • Lifetime projects to age 80 using the date of birth in Settings, and labels points by whole age.
  • Money / Time switches every figure between dollars saved and hours saved, using the daily cost and daily minutes you set per streak.
Why is a streak missing from part of the chart?

A streak only appears from its start date onward. Before that day it has saved nothing, so it has no dot, no label value, and no legend value at that point in the scrub.

03 — Waves, lapses & check-ins

The day-to-day tools

What is a wave?

A wave is an urge you log and ride out in the app instead of acting on. Logging the triggers connected to it is what builds the patterns Circuit shows you back in History. Completing a wave earns XP.

How do check-ins, XP, and trophies work?

Check in daily to record the day. Check-ins, completed waves, and clean-day milestones earn XP and unlock trophies. Life-area ratings you save on Home appear as trends on Progress.

Can I remove a trigger or an entry I logged by mistake?

Yes — entries you can edit or remove, such as trigger logs, are controlled in History inside the app. Deleting an entry is immediate and cannot be undone, because there is no server copy to restore from.

04 — Notifications

Local reminders, controlled by you

Circuit’s reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device — nothing is pushed from a server. If a reminder does not arrive, work through these in order:

  1. Open Circuit’s settings and confirm reminders are on. Use the test notification button there to check delivery.
  2. Open your phone’s system settings for Circuit and confirm notifications are allowed.
  3. Check Focus / Do Not Disturb — a scheduled Focus can hold local notifications back until it ends.
How do I turn reminders off?

Turn them off in Circuit’s settings, or block them for Circuit in your phone’s system settings. Turning reminders off never deletes your streaks or history.

05 — Money & time saved

Estimates from your numbers

Every saved figure is simple arithmetic: the daily cost or daily minutes you set for a streak, multiplied over time. The line is straight — it is not compounded, invested, or a forecast of your finances.

My saved total looks wrong. Where do I check?

Open the streak and check its start date and start time and its daily cost and daily minutes. The totals are only as accurate as those inputs — correct the input and the totals, including projections, recalculate.

How does the Lifetime figure work?

Lifetime projects your daily rates forward to age 80, using the date of birth stored in Settings. It assumes you stay clean and your daily cost stays the same. You can change your date of birth in Settings at any time — it stays on this device and only changes the projection.

06 — Your data & deletion

On your phone, under your control

Circuit stores streaks, check-ins, lapses, triggers, ratings, your date of birth, and your settings locally on your device. No account is created and nothing is sent to a Circuit server. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?
  • On this device: delete the app’s data in system settings, or uninstall Circuit. That removes the locally stored copy on that phone.
  • In a device backup: if you use iCloud Backup or Google backup, that backup may include Circuit’s data under your own account. Delete or refresh the backup in your iCloud / Google settings to remove it there too.

Deletion is final. We hold no server copy, so deleted data cannot be recovered — by you or by us.

I got a new phone. Where did my streaks go?

Circuit has no cloud sync and no account to sign back into. Your data moves only with a device backup you made — restoring your old phone’s iCloud or Google backup onto the new phone brings the app’s local data with it. Without a backup, there is no copy to restore.

Can someone else see what I logged?

Only someone who can unlock your phone and open the app. Your data inherits your device’s protection — passcode, Face ID / fingerprint, and encryption — so keep your device locked.

07 — Purchases & refunds

Handled by Apple and Google

Circuit is free to download. If paid features or a subscription are offered in the version you use, the store — Apple for the App Store, Google for Google Play — processes the payment, renewals, cancellations, and refunds under its own terms. Circuit never sees your full card number.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in the app while signed into the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. Because Circuit has no account of its own, a purchase cannot be moved between an Apple account and a Google account.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Cancel in your store account settings — on iPhone, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do refunds and free trials work?

Refund requests go to Apple or Google under the store’s refund policy — the publisher cannot issue store refunds directly. A free trial, if offered, converts to the paid price shown on the purchase screen unless you cancel before the trial ends.

08 — Troubleshooting

When something looks off

The app forgot my data after a reinstall

Uninstalling removes the app’s locally stored data from that device, and there is no server copy. The only way back is restoring a device backup (iCloud or Google) made before the uninstall.

A count, ring, or total does not match what I expect

Check the streak’s start date and start time, and its daily cost and minutes. Days complete at the start time each day, not at midnight, so a count that looks a few hours “behind” is usually the 24-hour cycle working as designed.

The app will not open or keeps closing

Restart the phone, then check the App Store or Google Play for a Circuit update and install it. If it still fails, note your phone model, OS version, and what you tapped last — and include those three things if you leave feedback (see below). Do not uninstall as a first step: uninstalling deletes your local data.

09 — Crisis & professional help

Circuit is support, not treatment

Circuit provides self-tracking, education, and encouragement. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, it is not therapy, and it is not a crisis service. Resources listed in the app are information only, not a referral or an endorsement.

If you are in crisis If you may harm yourself or someone else, call or text 988 in the United States (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline), call your local emergency number, or go to your nearest emergency department. Talk to your own clinician about treatment decisions, medication, and dosing.
